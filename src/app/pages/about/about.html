ABOUT FARF

Rooted in rural realities.
Working towards inclusive development.

Fundamental Action and Research Foundation is a non-profit organisation working in rural development, agriculture, education and research.

WHO WE ARE

Community priorities guide our work.

FARF focuses on supporting socially, educationally and economically disadvantaged communities through community development, digital services, government scheme awareness, rural education and sustainable development initiatives.

Its work includes community engagement, rural surveys, agricultural awareness, education support, digital facilitation and initiatives that connect citizens with relevant services and opportunities.

01 OUR MISSION

Serve needs. Expand access.

To serve the basic needs and priorities of socially, educationally and economically disadvantaged communities through inclusive development, awareness, research and access to essential services.

02 OUR VISION

Inclusive and sustainable rural development.

To contribute towards inclusive and sustainable rural development by empowering communities through education, awareness, opportunities, research and access to essential services.
