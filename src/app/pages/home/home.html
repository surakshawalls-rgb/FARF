WHO WE ARE

Working alongside communities for a more inclusive rural future.

Fundamental Action and Research Foundation (FARF) is a non-profit organisation working in rural development, agriculture, education and research.

Our work supports socially, educationally and economically disadvantaged communities through community engagement, digital services, government scheme awareness, rural education and sustainable development initiatives.
