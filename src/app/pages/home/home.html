ABOUT FARF

Working Together for Stronger Villages

Fundamental Action and Research Foundation (FARF) is a Bengaluru-based non-profit working towards rural development, agriculture, research, digital inclusion and public welfare.

👥

Our Approach

FARF works directly with rural communities to understand local challenges, create awareness, connect people with government schemes and digital services, and support village-level development.

Our objective is to make information, services and opportunities easier to access for people living in rural and underserved communities.

👁
OUR VISION

Empowering People. Strengthening Villages.

To serve the basic needs and priorities of socially, educationally and economically backward sections of society while creating awareness, transparency and opportunities at the grassroots level.
