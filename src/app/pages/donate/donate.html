ABOUT YOUR SUPPORT

Partner with FARF for Rural Development

FARF has been working among under-resourced communities in Eastern Uttar Pradesh, primarily in Bhadohi and Jaunpur District, since 2015.

Our vision is rural development in India, focusing on child education, skill development, financial inclusion, rural elderly welfare, digital services, government entitlements, and developmental research.

Through the Common Service Center, FARF helps weaker sections of rural areas access various government schemes and digital services.
